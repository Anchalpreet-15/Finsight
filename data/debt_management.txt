DEBT MANAGEMENT FOR INDIANS — EMI Se Azaadi

Debt is emotionally heavy. In India, EMI culture has made it easy to over-borrow without realising the true cost. Here's how to understand, manage, and get out of debt.

TYPES OF DEBT IN INDIA (by urgency)
HIGH PRIORITY (tackle first — most expensive):
- Credit card outstanding balance: 36–42% annual interest if you only pay the minimum. The most dangerous debt in India.
- Personal loans from banks/NBFCs: 12–24% interest per year
- Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) apps like LazyPay, ZestMoney, Simpl — interest kicks in fast if not cleared on time

MEDIUM PRIORITY:
- Car loans: 8–12% interest per year
- Consumer durable loans (for TV, phone, fridge): 0% EMI schemes often have hidden processing fees

LOWER PRIORITY (but still important):
- Home loans: 8.5–9.5% interest — tax-deductible under Section 24 (up to ₹2 lakh interest deduction) and Section 80C (up to ₹1.5 lakh principal repayment)
- Education loans: moratorium period applies; interest may be tax-deductible under Section 80E

CREDIT CARD DEBT — THE MOST URGENT PROBLEM
If you carry a credit card balance, you are paying 3–3.5% interest per MONTH (36–42% per year). Key actions:
- STOP adding new charges to the card you're paying off
- Pay MORE than the minimum — minimum payments are designed to keep you in debt for years
- Call your bank and request a conversion of outstanding balance to EMI at lower interest (most banks offer this)
- Consider a balance transfer to a card with 0% promotional rate or lower interest
- NEVER use one credit card to pay off another unless the interest rate is significantly lower

THE DEBT AVALANCHE METHOD (saves most money)
List all debts by interest rate, highest first. Pay minimum on all, throw every extra rupee at the highest-interest debt. Once cleared, roll that payment to the next. Mathematically optimal — saves the most interest over time.

THE DEBT SNOWBALL METHOD (keeps you motivated)
List all debts by outstanding amount, smallest first. Clear the smallest balance first, regardless of interest rate. The psychological wins of eliminating debts keep you going. Recommended for those feeling overwhelmed.

PERSONAL LOANS IN INDIA
- Never take a personal loan to fund lifestyle (vacation, gadgets, wedding expenses beyond budget)
- Acceptable uses: medical emergency when no other option exists, or consolidating higher-interest debt at a lower rate
- Check your CIBIL score before applying — above 750 gets you better rates
- Compare rates on BankBazaar, Paisabazaar before accepting any loan offer

HOME LOANS
- Prepay when you receive bonuses or windfall income — even ₹50,000 extra per year reduces your tenure significantly
- Home loan interest deduction up to ₹2 lakh under Section 24(b)
- Principal repayment up to ₹1.5 lakh qualifies under Section 80C

AVOID THESE DEBT TRAPS
- Chit funds with unreliable operators — join only SEBI-registered chit funds or avoid entirely
- Loan apps (instant loan apps) — many charge predatory interest rates of 30–100%+ annually
- Borrowing from moneylenders (sahukar) in rural/semi-urban areas — interest can be exploitative
- Using home loan top-ups for non-home expenses repeatedly

IF DEBT FEELS UNMANAGEABLE
- Contact your bank — most have restructuring or moratorium options
- RBI has mandated that banks must not harass borrowers; know your rights
- Credit counselling is available through organisations like MFIN (for microfinance) or your bank's financial wellness team
- Bankruptcy/insolvency (IBC process) exists as a last resort — not a moral failure, a legal fresh start

One EMI at a time. List your debts, pick a strategy, and begin. Even ₹500 extra per month towards debt makes a real difference over time.
