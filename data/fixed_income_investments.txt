FIXED INCOME INVESTMENTS IN INDIA — COMPLETE GUIDE

PPF (PUBLIC PROVIDENT FUND):
Current interest rate: 7.1% per annum (reviewed quarterly by Govt)
Tax status: EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) — contribution, interest, and maturity ALL tax-free
Section 80C: Annual contribution up to ₹1,50,000 qualifies
Lock-in period: 15 years (can extend in 5-year blocks indefinitely)
Minimum contribution: ₹500/year. Maximum: ₹1,50,000/year
Partial withdrawal: Allowed from Year 7 (up to 50% of balance at end of year 4)
Loan against PPF: Year 3-6 (up to 25% of end of 2nd preceding year balance)
Where to open: SBI, Post Office, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak (online/offline)
Best for: Long-term tax-free corpus, retirement, children's education
PPF interest: Calculated on minimum balance between 5th and last of month — deposit before 5th each month

EPF (EMPLOYEE PROVIDENT FUND):
Interest rate: 8.25% (FY 2024-25) — announced annually
Employer contributes 12% of basic salary; employee contributes 12%
Employer's 8.33% goes to EPS (pension), 3.67% to EPF
Tax-free on withdrawal after 5 years of continuous service
UAN (Universal Account Number) — portable across employers
Voluntary PF (VPF): Extra employee contribution beyond 12% (same interest rate as EPF)
Withdrawal: Full on retirement/death/permanent incapacity. Partial allowed for house, medical, marriage, education

NPS (NATIONAL PENSION SYSTEM):
Market-linked pension scheme; Tier 1 (mandatory, tax-exempt) and Tier 2 (voluntary, no tax benefit)
Allocation: Active choice (equity max 75%; up to 50% equity above age 50) or Auto choice (LC-75/LC-50/LC-25)
Tax benefits: 80CCD(1) — up to ₹1.5L (within 80C limit); 80CCD(1B) — extra ₹50K; 80CCD(2) — employer NPS up to 10% of basic (no limit, very tax-efficient)
Maturity: 60% lump sum (tax-free); 40% mandatory annuity (taxable)
Exit before 60: Only 20% tax-free lump sum; 80% to annuity
Lock-in: Till age 60 (partial withdrawal allowed after 3 years for specific purposes)
Regulated by PFRDA. Can be opened at banks, post offices, online at eNPS

FIXED DEPOSITS (FD):
Banks: SBI 6.5-7.1%, HDFC 6.6-7.25%, ICICI 6.7-7.25%, Kotak 6.2-7.4%
Small Finance Banks (AU, Ujjivan, ESAF, Suryoday): 8.0-9.5% (higher risk but DICGC insured up to ₹5L)
Senior citizen: Additional 0.25-0.5% on most banks
TDS: 10% if interest > ₹40,000/year (₹50,000 for seniors). Submit 15G/15H to avoid
Tax: Interest is taxable at income slab rate (not capital gain)
Compounding: Quarterly compounding is standard in India
Premature withdrawal: Usually allowed with 1% penalty on rate
Sweep-in FD: Links to savings account — auto-FD for idle cash, instant liquidity

RECURRING DEPOSITS (RD):
Banks: 5.5-7.5% typically
Post Office RD: 6.7% (guaranteed by govt, no TDS)
Duration: 6 months to 10 years
Tax: Interest taxable at slab rate; TDS applies if annual interest > ₹40K

BONDS:
Government Securities (G-Secs): 6.5-7.5%; safest; sovereign guarantee; tradeable
RBI Floating Rate Bonds: Currently 8.05% (linked to NSS rate + 0.35%); 7-year lock-in; taxable
Tax-Free Bonds: NHAI, REC, PFC issued (not currently available fresh; trade on exchanges at 4-6%)
Corporate Bonds: Higher yield (8-12%) but credit risk; CRISIL/ICRA rated; available on stock exchanges
54EC Bonds (NHAI/REC): For capital gains exemption on property sale; ₹50L limit; 5-year lock-in; 5.75%

SOVEREIGN GOLD BONDS (SGBs):
Issued by RBI; 2.5% annual interest (taxable); price linked to gold
Maturity: 8 years (can exit from 5th year on interest payment dates)
Tax-free on maturity; LTCG tax applies if sold on exchange before maturity
Best gold investment option: No storage cost, earns interest, tax-free on maturity
SGB series: RBI issues new series periodically (check RBI website)

SAVINGS INSTRUMENTS:
National Savings Certificate (NSC): 7.7%; 5-year lock-in; qualifies for 80C; Post Office
Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP): 7.5%; doubles money in ~115 months; Post Office; no 80C benefit
Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS): 8.2%; for 60+ (50+ if retired); max ₹30L; 80C eligible; quarterly interest
Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY): 8.2%; for girl child below 10; max ₹1.5L/year; EEE tax status; till girl turns 21
Post Office Monthly Income Scheme (POMIS): 7.4%; monthly income; max ₹9L single/₹15L joint; no 80C; 5-year

LIQUID FUNDS AND MONEY MARKET:
Liquid Mutual Funds: 7-8% return; can withdraw in 1 working day; no exit load after 7 days
Best for emergency fund, parking surplus salary
Ultra Short Duration / Money Market Funds: 7-8.5%; slightly better than liquid; 1-3 month horizon
Arbitrage Funds: ~6-7% return but taxed as equity (LTCG 12.5% after 1 year); good for short-term in high tax bracket
