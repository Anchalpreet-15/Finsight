CAPITAL GAINS, CIBIL SCORE & HRA — COMPREHENSIVE INDIA GUIDE

=== CAPITAL GAINS TAX (Updated Budget 2024/2025) ===

EQUITY & EQUITY MUTUAL FUNDS:
Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) — held more than 1 year:
- Tax rate: 12.5% on gains exceeding ₹1,25,000 in a financial year
- No indexation benefit
- If total LTCG in year < ₹1,25,000 → ZERO tax
- Tax loss harvesting: Book gains up to ₹1.25L each March to reset cost basis

Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG) — held less than 1 year:
- Tax rate: 20% (increased from 15% in Budget 2024)
- No exemptions; fully taxable

ELSS mutual funds: Same as equity — 1-year LTCG, gains above ₹1.25L taxed at 12.5%

REAL ESTATE (Budget 2024 major change):
LTCG — held more than 2 years:
- Tax rate: 12.5% WITHOUT indexation (indexation benefit removed Budget 2024)
- Previously: 20% with indexation (old regime more beneficial for old properties)
- Option: Taxpayers can choose old regime (20% + indexation) for properties bought before July 23, 2024
- Section 54 exemption: Reinvest in another residential property within 1 year (before) or 2 years (after sale) or 3 years (under construction)
- Section 54EC: Invest up to ₹50L in NHAI/REC bonds within 6 months — entire LTCG exempt

STCG on real estate — held less than 2 years: Taxed at income slab rate

DEBT MUTUAL FUNDS (post April 1, 2023):
- ALL gains (regardless of holding period) are now taxed at income slab rate
- Indexation and LTCG benefits completely removed for debt funds
- Better alternatives now: FD (similar tax), SGBs, direct bonds

GOLD:
Physical gold, Gold ETF, Gold Fund of Funds:
- LTCG (held >2 years): 12.5% (Budget 2024)
- STCG (held <2 years): Slab rate
Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB):
- Interest (2.5%): Taxable at slab rate
- On maturity (8 years) from RBI: Completely TAX-FREE
- If sold on exchange before maturity: LTCG at 12.5% (after 1 year holding)

CRYPTO AND VIRTUAL DIGITAL ASSETS (VDA):
- Flat 30% tax on all gains (no slab, no exemption)
- 1% TDS on every transaction above ₹50,000
- Losses CANNOT be set off against any other income
- Losses from one crypto CANNOT offset gains from another crypto

TAX LOSS HARVESTING STRATEGY:
- Sell loss-making investments before March 31
- STCL can offset STCG and LTCG
- LTCL can only offset LTCG
- Carry forward unused losses for 8 years (file ITR to claim)
- For equity: Book ₹1.25L LTCG every March (tax-free) to reset cost basis — save significant tax over time

=== CIBIL SCORE & CREDIT HEALTH ===

CIBIL SCORE RANGES:
- 750-900: Excellent — Best loan rates, instant approvals
- 700-749: Good — Loans approved, slightly higher rates
- 650-699: Fair — Loans possible but higher rates (1-3% more)
- 600-649: Poor — Difficult to get loans; only NBFC/high-rate options
- 300-599: Very Poor — Loan rejection; need urgent credit repair

HOW CIBIL SCORE IS CALCULATED:
1. Payment History (35%): Most important factor. Even ONE missed payment drops score 50-100 points.
2. Credit Utilisation (30%): Keep below 30% of total credit limit. Ideal: under 10%.
   Example: If total credit limit = ₹1,00,000, keep outstanding below ₹30,000.
3. Credit Age/History Length (15%): Older credit history = better score. Keep oldest card active.
4. Credit Mix (10%): Mix of secured (home loan, car loan) + unsecured (CC, personal loan) is good.
5. New Credit Enquiries (10%): Hard enquiries reduce score. Don't apply for multiple loans/cards.

COMMON CREDIT SCORE MISTAKES:
- Paying only minimum due on credit card (still increases utilisation, interest piles up)
- Closing old credit cards (reduces credit age and total limit)
- Multiple loan applications in short time (each hard enquiry hurts score)
- Missing EMI even by 1 day can be reported after 30+ days overdue
- Settling a loan for less than owed (shows as "Settled" not "Closed" — hurts for 7 years)

HOW TO IMPROVE CIBIL SCORE:
- Pay ALL EMIs and credit card dues BEFORE due date (set up auto-pay)
- Reduce credit card utilisation below 30% immediately
- Request credit limit increase from bank (improves utilisation ratio without spending more)
- Get a secured credit card against FD if score is very low
- Dispute errors in CIBIL report (free at cibil.com — 1 free report per year; also Paisabazaar/BankBazaar)
- Don't close old credit cards unless annual fee is very high
- Add yourself as authorized user on a family member's good credit card

CREDIT CARD BEST PRACTICES:
- Pay FULL outstanding amount before due date (not just minimum)
- Use below 30% of credit limit each month
- Don't take cash advance (2.5-3.5% fee + interest from day 1)
- Compare reward cards: HDFC Regalia, Axis Magnus, SBI SimplyCLICK, Amex MRCC
- International transactions: 1.5-3.5% forex markup fee
- Credit card interest: 36-42% per annum if dues not paid fully — wealth destroyer

LOAN AGAINST SECURITIES (LAS):
- Overdraft against MF, shares, FD, insurance policies
- Lower interest than personal loan (9-13%)
- Interest only on amount used
- Emergency use when you don't want to break investments

=== HRA (HOUSE RENT ALLOWANCE) EXEMPTION ===

AVAILABILITY: Only under OLD Tax Regime (not available in New Regime)

CALCULATION — MINIMUM OF THREE:
1. Actual HRA received from employer
2. 50% of basic salary (if staying in Delhi/Mumbai/Kolkata/Chennai — metro cities)
   OR 40% of basic salary (all other cities — non-metro)
3. Actual rent paid MINUS 10% of basic salary

EXAMPLE:
Basic salary: ₹40,000/month (₹4,80,000/year)
HRA received: ₹20,000/month (₹2,40,000/year)
Rent paid: ₹18,000/month (₹2,16,000/year)
City: Bangalore (non-metro — 40% rule)

a) Actual HRA: ₹2,40,000
b) 40% of basic: ₹1,92,000
c) Rent - 10% basic: ₹2,16,000 - ₹48,000 = ₹1,68,000

Minimum = ₹1,68,000 → This is the tax-free HRA exemption
Taxable HRA: ₹2,40,000 - ₹1,68,000 = ₹72,000

METRO CITIES for HRA (50% rule): Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai
NON-METRO (40% rule): Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad, and all other cities

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Rent receipts (monthly for rent > ₹3,000)
- Rental agreement
- Landlord's PAN if annual rent > ₹1,00,000
- You CAN pay rent to parents — need rent agreement + bank transfer proof

HRA WHEN LIVING IN OWN HOUSE: No HRA exemption. But can claim home loan interest deduction (₹2L u/s 24b).
HRA + HOME LOAN: Can claim both if working city is different from property location.

LEAVE TRAVEL ALLOWANCE (LTA):
- For domestic travel only (not international)
- Economy class airfare or AC first class train fare (actual ticket cost)
- 2 trips in a block of 4 years (current block: 2022-2025)
- Maximum equal to actual travel cost
- Only available in old tax regime
- Submit travel proof (tickets, boarding passes) to employer
