INSURANCE GUIDE FOR INDIANS

WHY INSURANCE MATTERS:
Insurance protects your financial plan from being destroyed by unexpected events. Without proper insurance, a single medical emergency or death can wipe out years of savings.

TERM LIFE INSURANCE:
Pure protection — pays a lump sum to your family if you die during the policy term.
Recommended coverage: 10 to 15 times your annual income
Example: If you earn ₹10 lakh/year → get ₹1 crore to ₹1.5 crore cover
Premium: Very affordable. ₹1 crore cover costs approximately ₹8,000-₹12,000/year for a 30-year-old non-smoker for 30-year term.
Best companies: LIC Tech Term, HDFC Life Click2Protect, ICICI Prudential iProtect, Max Life Smart Secure Plus, Tata AIA
When to buy: As early as possible. Premium increases with age.
Who needs it: Anyone with financial dependents (spouse, children, aging parents, outstanding loans)

HEALTH INSURANCE:
Medical costs in India are rising 15-20% annually. One hospitalization can cost ₹3-10 lakh.
Recommended: Minimum ₹5 lakh individual cover; ₹10-20 lakh family floater
Types:
- Individual: Covers one person
- Family Floater: One policy covers entire family, sum insured shared
- Super Top-up: Additional cover above a threshold, very affordable
Best companies: Niva Bupa, Care Health, HDFC Ergo, Star Health, Aditya Birla Health
Key features to check: No room rent capping, no co-payment, cashless hospitals nearby, restoration benefit, no claim bonus
Corporate health insurance from employer is NOT enough — it stops when you leave the job.

TERM VS ULIP:
ULIP (Unit Linked Insurance Plan) combines insurance + investment. This is generally NOT recommended.
- High charges (premium allocation, policy administration, mortality)
- Returns often lower than pure mutual funds
- Lock-in of 5 years
Better approach: Buy term insurance (pure protection, cheap) + invest separately in mutual funds

VEHICLE INSURANCE:
- Third-party insurance: Mandatory by law for all vehicles
- Comprehensive insurance: Covers your vehicle damage too — highly recommended
- Zero depreciation add-on: Recommended for new vehicles (up to 3 years)
- Return to Invoice add-on: Covers full invoice value in case of total loss

CRITICAL ILLNESS INSURANCE:
Pays a lump sum on diagnosis of serious illness (cancer, heart attack, stroke).
Useful because treatment takes months and income stops while expenses rise.
Cover ₹25-50 lakh. Can be bought as standalone or add-on with health insurance.

INSURANCE TRAPS TO AVOID:
1. Never mix insurance and investment (avoid traditional endowment plans, money-back policies)
2. Don't buy insurance to save tax — buy for protection, tax benefit is secondary
3. Don't under-insure because you want to save on premium
4. Always disclose pre-existing conditions — hiding them can lead to claim rejection
5. Read the policy document — especially exclusions

HOW MUCH TO SPEND ON INSURANCE:
Total insurance premium should not exceed 5% of annual income
Prioritise: Term life first, then health insurance, then everything else
