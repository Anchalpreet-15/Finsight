MUTUAL FUNDS COMPLETE GUIDE FOR INDIA

WHAT IS A MUTUAL FUND:
A mutual fund pools money from many investors and invests in stocks, bonds, or other securities. A professional fund manager manages the portfolio.

HOW SIP WORKS:
SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) = Investing a fixed amount every month automatically.
Power of compounding: ₹5,000/month for 20 years at 12% return = ₹49.5 lakh maturity on ₹12 lakh invested.
Start early: ₹5,000/month started at 25 gives more than ₹10,000/month started at 35.

TYPES OF MUTUAL FUNDS BY ASSET CLASS:
Equity Funds (market-linked, for long-term 5+ years):
- Large Cap: Invest in top 100 companies by market cap. Stable, less risky.
- Mid Cap: Companies ranked 101-250. Higher growth, more volatile.
- Small Cap: Companies below rank 250. Highest growth potential, highest risk.
- Multi Cap / Flexi Cap: Mix of large, mid, small — diversified.
- ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme): Tax-saving under 80C, 3-year lock-in.
- Index Funds: Passively track Nifty 50 or Sensex. Low cost, good for beginners.

Debt Funds (lower risk, for short to medium term):
- Liquid Funds: For emergency fund, park money for 1 day to 3 months.
- Short Duration / Corporate Bond: For 1-3 year goals.
- Gilt Funds: Government securities, safe but interest rate sensitive.

Hybrid Funds (mix of equity + debt):
- Aggressive Hybrid: 65-80% equity + 20-35% debt. Good for moderate risk.
- Balanced Advantage (BAF): Dynamically adjusts equity-debt based on market valuations.

RECOMMENDED FUNDS FOR BEGINNERS:
- Nifty 50 Index Fund (Nippon, UTI, HDFC) — low cost, diversified
- Nifty Next 50 Index Fund — mid-large exposure
- Large & Mid Cap Fund — balanced growth

HOW TO INVEST:
Platforms: Zerodha Coin, Groww, Paytm Money, ET Money, MF Central (direct plans, no commission)
Direct vs Regular: Direct plans have no distributor commission → higher returns by 0.5-1% per year
Go direct unless you genuinely need advice.

KEY TERMS:
NAV (Net Asset Value): Price of one unit of the fund. Changes daily.
AUM (Assets Under Management): Total money managed. Larger AUM = more stable.
Expense Ratio: Annual fee charged by the fund. Lower is better. Index funds: 0.05-0.2%; Active funds: 0.5-2%.
Exit Load: Penalty for redeeming early. Usually 1% if redeemed within 1 year for equity funds.

SIP TIPS:
- Set up auto-debit on salary day
- Never stop SIP during market falls — buy more units at lower prices
- Increase SIP by 10% every year (SIP step-up)
- Don't track NAV daily — it causes anxiety and bad decisions
- Stay invested for minimum 5 years for equity funds

TAX ON MUTUAL FUNDS (from FY 2023-24):
Equity funds:
- STCG (< 1 year): 15% flat
- LTCG (> 1 year): 10% on gains above ₹1,00,000 per year
Debt funds:
- All gains taxed at your income tax slab rate (indexation benefit removed from FY 2023-24)
ELSS:
- LTCG applies (3-year mandatory lock-in)
- Tax-deductible under Section 80C up to ₹1.5 lakh

MISTAKES TO AVOID:
1. Investing in too many funds (5-6 is enough)
2. Stopping SIP when market falls (do the opposite — increase SIP)
3. Choosing funds based on last 1-year returns only
4. Keeping money in savings account instead of liquid fund (6-7% vs 3%)
5. Redeeming for short-term needs from equity funds
